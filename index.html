ABOUT ME

A little bit of everything, on purpose

I'm a UI/UX designer who genuinely loves what I do. I started learning design back in high school and haven't stopped since. I believe design isn't just about making things look pretty, it's the best way to express how you feel and tell a story. I'm an extrovert at heart, so I thrive in team settings where ideas bounce around and something bigger gets built together. I also dabble in graphic design, frontend coding, and songwriting, because when I don't have words to say out loud, I put them in a song instead.

What I care about

Building things that feel warm and human, even when they're technical — clean interfaces, thoughtful code, and honest storytelling.
